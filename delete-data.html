NWM Admin — Request deletion of your account and data

NWM Admin (North West Mission Administration) · published by Bethel Apps

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to bethelediting@gmail.com with the subject “NWM Admin – Delete my data”.
  2. Include your full name, your NWM role and section, and the mobile number or email address you use to sign in to NWM Admin.
  3. We will confirm your request with NWM leadership and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, your mobile number / email address, your profile photo, and photos or files you uploaded that are not part of official records.

What may be kept

Official NWM records needed for mission administration — for example approved reports, mission calendar records and official communications already delivered — may be kept, with your name, for NWM's record keeping.
