NWM Admin — Privacy Policy

North West Mission Administration · Last updated: 9 October 2026

NWM Admin is the internal administration app of the North West Mission (“NWM”), published by Bethel Apps. It is used only by NWM's District Leaders, Section Leaders and Functional Team Leaders, whose accounts are set up by NWM leadership. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Identity and role: your name, your NWM role (District Leader, Section Leader, Functional Team Leader, NWM Admin) and your section.
  • Contact details: your mobile number and/or email address, used for sign-in and so other NWM leaders can contact you.
  • Photos and files you choose to add: profile photos, event posters and photos, report photos, and images or PDF documents attached to official communications.
  • Content you create: mission calendar events, reports, community posts and comments, and NWM official communications.
  • Activity records: approvals, returns and rejections in the review workflows, and whether an official communication or notification has been read.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, health or financial information, or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

Only to run NWM administration: signing in, the leadership directory, the Mission Activity Calendar and its approvals, reports and record keeping, official communications, community posts and in-app notifications.

Call, WhatsApp and SMS

These buttons only open your phone's own dialer, WhatsApp or SMS app with a leader's registered number. NWM Admin does not record, store or read calls or messages made that way.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or other third parties. Data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) and is visible only to NWM members according to their role. There are no ads in the app.

Security

Data is transferred over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and protected by role-based access rules. Files are kept in private storage and opened only through short-lived links for the people allowed to see them.

Retention and deletion

Official records (for example approved reports, calendar records and official communications) are kept for NWM administration. To ask about, correct or delete your personal data or your account, contact us at the address below.

Children

NWM Admin is for adult church leaders only and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
